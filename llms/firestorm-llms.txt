# Firestorm

> Firestorm (Firestorm Labs, Inc.) is a San Diego based defense technology company
> founded in 2022. It builds expeditionary manufacturing systems and modular unmanned
> aerial systems: the xCELL containerized production unit 3D prints industrial-grade
> PA-12 nylon composite airframes and spare parts at the tactical edge, feeding the
> Tempest modular fixed-wing UAS family and the Squall additively-manufactured FPV
> drone. Firestorm ships hardware. It publishes no developer portal, no public API,
> no SDK, and no machine-readable API contract.
